## 1. Project Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

### Problem
Solana developer onboarding is the single most critical driver for ecosystem growth. However, existing educational resources suffer from three main drawbacks:
1. **Lack of Verifiable Credentials**: Developers spend weeks building sample programs without any tamper-proof, on-chain proof of competence.
2. **Low Retention & Isolation**: Learning Rust and Anchor smart contracts in isolation leads to high drop-out rates without structured peer study groups and calendar integration.
3. **Monolithic & Uninteractive Content**: Static tutorials lack real-time code validation, interactive quizzes, and tangible incentives like token staking.

### Solution: ChainTree
ChainTree is an open-source, full-stack Solana Web3 developer platform that turns learning into verifiable on-chain assets:
- **Interactive Rust & Anchor Bootcamps**: Step-by-step modular tracks covering Anchor framework, Metaplex Bubblegum, and Web3.js client development.
- **On-Chain cNFT Credentials**: Upon completing 100% of a course, developers automatically mint Metaplex compressed NFTs (cNFTs) directly to their connected Solana wallet.
- **Public Credential Verifier**: Anyone—protocols, hiring managers, or hackathon judges—can verify developer certificates against Solana Devnet RPC with instant Solscan and Solana Explorer links.
- **Peer-to-Peer Study Cohorts**: Community study groups with scheduled meetups, live member rosters, Discord integration, and one-click `.ics` calendar exports.
- **Anchor Staking Vault**: Developers stake TREE tokens to lock in commitment streaks, boost XP multipliers, earn yields, and request Devnet SOL airdrops.
